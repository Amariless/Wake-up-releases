## What it is

Wake up keeps everything you need for the semester in one app: your classes, your schedule, your assignments and alarms designed to actually get you out of bed. To turn one off you have to solve a small challenge, so there's no switching it off without opening your eyes.

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>Plan your semester</h3>
      <p>Create folders for a semester, a course or anything you like, with subjects, a schedule for each day and the room where each class takes place.</p>
      <p>When the semester ends, close it with one tap: its alarms and reminders turn off and the history stays.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>Never miss a deadline</h3>
      <p>Every assignment can have a due date, with reminders a week before, a day before and on the day itself. You choose when.</p>

### Challenges to turn off an alarm

| Challenge | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Shake** | Shake your phone |
| **Math** | Solve a math problem |
| **Dots** | Connect the dots in order |
| **Trace** | Follow a curved line with your finger |
| **Type** | Write a phrase exactly as shown |

## Download

Wake up isn't on the Play Store; you install it from the APK. It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

1. [Download `wake-up.apk`](https://github.com/Amariless/Wake-up-releases/releases/latest/download/wake-up.apk) on your phone and open it.
2. Android will ask you to allow your browser or file manager to install apps. That's normal for apps that don't come from the Play Store.
3. If Google Play Protect says the app isn't verified, choose *Install anyway*.

New versions install from inside the app, in *Settings → Check for updates*. They install over the previous one without deleting your folders, assignments, alarms or settings. All versions and their notes are on the [Releases](https://github.com/Amariless/Wake-up-releases/releases) page.

## Permissions

The app has a guide in *Settings → Permissions* that walks you through each one. Only the first three are needed for alarms to work. The rest turn on specific features, and you can skip them if you don't use those features.

| Permission | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Notifications** | Required. Alarm, assignment and screen time notices. |
| **Exact alarms** | Required. So alarms ring right on time, not minutes later. |
| **Full screen** | Required. So the alarm shows up over the lock screen. |
| **No battery optimization** | Highly recommended. Keeps Android from closing the app and missing alarms. |
| **App usage** and **installed apps list** | Only for screen time. |
| **Display over other apps** | Only for the "limit reached" notice on top of Instagram or TikTok. |
| **Accessibility** | Only for the Reels and TikTok limit. More below. |
| **Internet** and **install apps** | Only to check for and install updates from this repository. |

<details>
<summary><b>About the accessibility permission</b></summary>
<br>

It's optional. Without it, the app works fully and only the Reels and TikTok limit and the social media notices are turned off. The app uses it only to know whether you're watching Reels or TikTok: it doesn't read your messages or anything else, and it never touches your direct messages.

Everything happens on your phone. The app has no server, ads or analytics, and only uses the internet to check for updates. You can remove the permission anytime in *Android settings → Accessibility*.

Google Play doesn't allow this use of accessibility, which is why Wake up is shared as an APK.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>If you have a Xiaomi phone</b></summary>
<br>

MIUI and HyperOS close background apps very aggressively. To make sure alarms always ring, turn on **Autostart** and **Background pop-ups** (in *Security → Permissions*) and set Wake up's battery use to **No restrictions**.

</details>
